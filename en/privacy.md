# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Pocket GR (the "App") is an independently developed app for using Ricoh GR III / GR IIIx cameras from an iPhone or iPad. The App does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer or to any third party. There is no server, no account, no analytics, and no advertising.

## 1. Data handled only on your device

The following data travels only between your device and your camera and never leaves them.

- Location: the App sends your iPhone's GPS position to the camera over Bluetooth so the camera can record where each photo was taken. With "Background sync" on, it keeps sending while the App is in the background. The last position is stored on the device only, for the next transfer.
- Photos: the App receives photos over the camera's Wi-Fi network and, with your permission, saves them to an album in the Photos app. Photo metadata (EXIF) is read on the device only, to show it on screen.
- Camera details: the camera's name, serial number, pairing identifier, and the Wi-Fi access details the camera reports are stored on the device so the App can reconnect.
- Communication log: requests exchanged with the camera are kept in device memory only and are discarded when the App quits.

## 2. Permissions

- Bluetooth: camera power control, battery status, time sync, location transfer
- Location: sending the shooting location to the camera
- Local Network: photo transfer, live view, and remote shooting with a camera connected over Wi-Fi
- Add to Photos: saving received photos to the Photos app

You can turn any permission off at any time in iOS Settings. Only the related feature stops working.

## 3. Sharing with third parties

The App shares no data with third parties. It uses no third-party SDKs, analytics tools, or advertising networks.

## 4. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## 5. Deleting your data

Everything the App stores is removed when you delete the App. Photos saved to the Photos app are managed in the Photos app.

## 6. Changes

If this policy changes, this document is updated with the App and the effective date changes.
